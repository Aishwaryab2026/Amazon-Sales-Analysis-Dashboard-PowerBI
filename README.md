# Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard focuses on comparing sales performance across four key business dimensions:

- **Category**
- **Market**
- **Top 5 Countries**
- **Ship Mode**

The dashboard also provides an overall view of **Total Sales, Quantity, Profit, and Shipping Cost** through KPI cards.

The objective is to transform sales data into an easy-to-understand visual report that highlights differences in sales performance across products, regions, countries, and shipping methods.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to analyze and compare sales performance across different business dimensions and identify the strongest contributors to overall sales.

The analysis answers questions such as:

- Which product category generates the highest sales?
- Which market contributes the most to sales?
- Which **Top 5 countries** have the highest sales?
- How do sales compare across different shipping modes?
- What is the overall sales, quantity, profit, and shipping-cost performance?

## 🔍 Sales Comparisons

### 1. Sales by Category

Sales are compared across three major product categories:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

**Technology** records the highest sales contribution among the three categories, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

This comparison helps identify which product category contributes most to overall sales.

---

### 2. Sales by Market

Sales are compared across different global markets:

- APAC
- EU
- US
- LATAM
- EMEA

This provides a regional perspective of sales performance and helps identify the markets contributing the most to overall sales.

---

### 3. Sales by Top 5 Countries

The dataset contains sales information for multiple countries. To make the comparison more focused, a **Top 5 filter based on sales** was applied.

The dashboard highlights the five best-performing countries with respect to sales:

- United States
- Australia
- France
- China
- Germany

This Top 5 analysis makes it easier to identify the strongest country-level contributors without displaying the entire list of countries.

---

### 4. Sales by Ship Mode

Sales are compared across different shipping modes:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

**Standard Class** contributes the largest share of sales among the available shipping modes.

This comparison provides insight into how sales are distributed across different delivery options.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Technology** is the highest-performing category by sales.
- **APAC** is the leading market in the displayed analysis.
- The **Top 5 country filter** highlights the strongest country-level contributors to sales.
- **Standard Class** has the highest sales contribution among the shipping modes.
- The dashboard records **12.64M in total sales** and **1.47M in total profit**.
- The combination of category, market, country, and ship-mode analysis provides multiple perspectives on overall sales performance.
